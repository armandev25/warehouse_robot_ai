FRAGILE ITEMS:
Robots must use low acceleration and reduced grip force.
Do not stack fragile items more than two layers.
Use padded grippers when available.

HEAVY ITEMS:
Before lifting, verify the gripper torque limits.
Ensure load distribution is centered.
Avoid transporting heavy items on inclined surfaces.

HAZARDOUS MATERIALS:
Perform safety scan before pickup.
Use sealed containment mode when transporting chemicals.
Keep distance from heat sources and sparks.

ROBOT GRIPPER SPECIFICATIONS:
Maximum payload capacity is 15 kg.
Grip force range is adjustable between 5N and 50N.
Soft-contact mode is recommended for delicate objects.

SAFETY CHECKS BEFORE MOVEMENT:
Confirm obstacle-free path.
Verify object is securely held.
Ensure emergency stop system is active.

TROUBLESHOOTING:
If object slips, reduce speed and increase grip pressure.
If detection fails, recalibrate camera exposure.
If arm vibration occurs, reduce movement speed.
